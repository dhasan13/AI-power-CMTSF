# CMTSF-Net Datasets Directory

This folder holds audio datasets for training and benchmarking:
- **ASVspoof 2021 DF & LA subsets**: Deepfake and logical access synthetic speech benchmarks.
- **VoxCeleb 1 & 2**: Real-world authentic human voice profiles across diverse telephone and interview acoustics.
- **ElevenLabs / Diffusion Vocoder Clones**: Commercial and open-source zero-shot voice clones.

*Note: In Phase 1, the live call monitor streams directly from your microphone or audio interface.*
